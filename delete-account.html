Delete your More Parties DC account

This page is for members of the More Parties DC app, published by MorePartiesDC LLC.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open More Parties DC and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom and confirm.

Or request deletion by email

Email hello@morepartiesdc.com from the address on your account with the subject Delete my account. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, name, email, phone number, Instagram handle, date of birth, preferences, Balloons balance, check-ins, RSVPs and table requests.

What may be kept

Records we must retain for legal, tax or safety reasons (for example a completed booking receipt or a venue incident report) are kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted. Backups are purged within 90 days.
